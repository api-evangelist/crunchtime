In the restaurant industry, the ultimate measure of any new technology is simple: does it help operators take better care of their guests? For Michael Matos, Senior Director of Ops Services at Logan’s Roadhouse, this question guides every technology investment. Logan’s Roadhouse, which operates more than 130 casual dining locations across 22 states, is leveraging AI not to automate hospitality, but to protect it.
